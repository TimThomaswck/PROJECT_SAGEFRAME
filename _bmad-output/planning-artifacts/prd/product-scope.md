# Product Scope

## MVP - Minimum Viable Product

The MVP is focused on delivering the core "empathetic co-pilot" experience and the foundational features for task and information management.

*   **The Jarvis Co-Pilot (Enhanced Communication):** The empathetic AI persona with its unique communication style.
*   **Mood-Aware Task Triage:** The mood check-in mechanism that suggests tasks based on user's energy levels.
*   **Proactive Social Scheduling:** Functionality to analyze calendar availability and suggest scheduling social and professional engagements.
*   **Intelligent Curation via Smart Tags:** The frictionless input system for capturing diverse information.
*   **Short Notes Ingestion:** The ability to digitize physical short notes via OCR.
*   **Core Task & Project Management:** Including Kanban/Gantt chart views for tasks and projects.
*   **Calendar Integration:** Bi-directional sync with major calendar providers.
