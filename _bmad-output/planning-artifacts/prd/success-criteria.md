# Success Criteria

## User Success

Success for our users is defined by a tangible sense of empowerment and progress, moving from a state of overwhelm to one of calm control and focused achievement.

*   **Empowerment through Progression:** Users feel a sense of accomplishment by tracking daily task completions and seeing their progress through a gamified leveling system with XP points awarded for task completion, complexity, and priority.
*   **Reduced Stress and Burnout:** Users report a measurable decrease in stress and burnout, validated by a 20% reduction in self-reported stress levels after three months of use.
*   **Trust in the Co-Pilot:** Users view the "Jarvis" AI as an essential and supportive partner in their daily routine, with 90%+ describing the persona as "helpful," "calm," or "supportive."
*   **Well-being Engagement:** When feeling down, users trust the system's suggestions, engaging with mood-boosting activities at least 50% of the time.

## Business Success

*   **Early Adoption & Engagement:** Within the first three months, achieve a core group of highly engaged daily active users who are early adopters and champions of the product.
*   **Social Feature Validation:** Demonstrate the value of the platform's social features by having new users successfully schedule at least one social or professional connection within their first week.
*   **Long-Term User Retention:** After 12 months, maintain a high retention rate, demonstrating that Sageframe has become an indispensable part of our users' lives.

## Technical Success

*   **Integration Reliability:** Core API integrations, especially bi-directional calendar sync, must maintain a 99.9% success rate.
*   **Platform Stability & Performance:** Both Windows and Android applications must be highly responsive and stable, with minimal crashes or performance issues.
*   **Visual Task Management:** The Kanban/Gantt chart views must be fluid, responsive, and provide a clear, intuitive way for users to manage their tasks and projects.

## Measurable Outcomes

*   90%+ user satisfaction with the AI persona.
*   50% engagement with well-being features during low moods.
*   20% reduction in user-reported stress after three months.
*   New users schedule at least one social connection in the first week.
