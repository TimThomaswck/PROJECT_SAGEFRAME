# User Journeys

## Alex's Journey: From Chaos to Calm Control (Updated)

*   **Persona:** Alex, a second-year university student juggling a full course load, a part-time job, and a social life. She's overwhelmed by deadlines and a chaotic system of planners and apps.
*   **Opening Scene:** We meet Alex in her dorm room, surrounded by sticky notes and feeling defeated after missing a deadline. Her current "system" is failing her, causing stress and anxiety.
*   **Rising Action:** On a friend's recommendation, Alex tries Sageframe. The calm "Jarvis-like" co-pilot guides her through syncing her calendar and digitizing her notes. She begins using the daily mood check-in.
*   **New: AI Activation Moment:** After logging several mood check-ins, Alex has 12 tasks piling up. Jarvis gently suggests: "I can help you prioritize these tasks based on your energy levels. Would you like to enable AI scheduling?" Alex is intrigued and clicks "Learn More."
*   **API Key Setup:** Following the simple 3-step guide, Alex creates a free Gemini API key in under 2 minutes. She pastes it into Sageframe's secure settings. Immediately, Jarvis analyzes her task list and says: "Based on your current 'anxious but motivated' state, I suggest starting with 2 medium-priority tasks this afternoon, saving the complex project work for tomorrow morning when you're fresher."
*   **Climax:** During a stressful mid-term week, Alex logs a low mood. Sageframe's **LLM-powered Jarvis** (now understanding her full context) doesn't just suggest templates—it reasons: "I see you're exhausted and have 3 hours today. Instead of that database assignment (which typically takes you 4-5 hours based on past tasks), let's finish the literature review (estimated 90 minutes) and pre-read for tomorrow's lecture (30 minutes). Save the database work for Saturday morning when you have a 4-hour block and usually feel energized." Alex feels truly understood.
*   **Resolution:** Weeks later, Alex is no longer overwhelmed. Jarvis feels like a supportive study partner who knows her patterns, respects her energy, and helps her make realistic progress. She trusts Sageframe with her entire academic and social life. The AI scheduling has become indispensable—she can't imagine going back to manual planning.

## Ben's Journey: Navigating the Early Career Maze

*   **Persona:** Ben, a recent graduate in his first job, looking to find a new role while managing his personal life.
*   **Opening Scene:** Ben is staring at a mess of browser tabs, a spreadsheet, and a half-finished cover letter. He just sent the wrong resume to a top company and feels disorganized and is losing opportunities.
*   **Rising Action:** Ben uses Sageframe to create a "Job Hunt" project. He builds a "Resume Repository" to easily tailor his resume for different applications and tracks each application as a task.
*   **Climax:** Ben gets an interview. In Sageframe, he has the specific resume he sent, the job description, and his notes all in one place. Sageframe sees the interview in his calendar and proactively suggests he block out time to prepare, linking him to his "Resume Repository". He feels confident and prepared.
*   **Resolution:** Ben gets the job. He continues to use Sageframe as his central life-management hub, archiving the "Job Hunt" project and creating new ones for his new role. He feels in command of both his professional and personal life.

## Journey Requirements Summary

*   **Alex's Journey** reveals the need for a seamless onboarding process, the "Jarvis" AI persona, bi-directional calendar integration, Short Notes Ingestion (OCR), Mood-Aware Task Triage, and Proactive Social Scheduling.
*   **Ben's Journey** highlights the necessity for robust project creation and management, a rich text editor suitable for a resume repository, task tracking capabilities, and intelligent calendar integration that aids in preparation.
